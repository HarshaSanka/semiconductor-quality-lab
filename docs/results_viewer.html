Research protocol

Fixed 80/20 stratified development–test split, seed 42. Five development folds share identical rows across experiments. Missingness filtering, median imputation, constant-feature removal, scaling, selection and model fitting occur inside each fold.

Pick the highest mean CV AP. Choose a threshold by maximizing F2 on the selected model's development out-of-fold scores. Evaluate that fixed model and threshold once on the holdout. Report stratified bootstrap intervals conditional on class counts.

What this study can support

A reproducible comparison of predictive methods on a small historical dataset. Chronological sensitivity checks use development data only and are not an independent prospective validation.

What needs further research

Anonymized sensors have no known physical meanings. Correlation and SHAP values do not establish causation. No process setpoint optimization, guaranteed yield improvement, production readiness or direct 3DHI/NGMM implementation is claimed. Correlated runs, drift, small failure counts and uncalibrated scores limit generalization.
